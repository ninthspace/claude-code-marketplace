## Notes

**NFR5 and AD5 are deliberately absent.** NFR5 ("`cpm:status` and `cpm:ralph` call the script; neither reimplements the union, the matching, or the state derivation") has no oracle in this epic — it is demonstrated by the consumers, so its rows belong to 44-02 and 44-03. AD5's testable half is the documented invocation running with `CLAUDE_PROJECT_DIR` unset, and that invocation lives in `status/SKILL.md` — FR6's criterion, in 44-02. Task 3.1 still sources the resolver so the consumer epics have something to assert against. Claiming either here would be a criterion naming a consumer that does not consume (retro 21, retro 22).

**Rows 25 and 26 were added by the Step 4 cross-epic gap check**, after rows 1–24 were confirmed. NFR4 had fallen through every epic: AD3 ("tab-separated records via `printf`") reads as though it covers the same ground, but AD3 says what the format *is* and NFR4 says the format must serve two readers without a second rendering. Row 26 (AD2) was added at the same time for consistency with the AD3, AD4 and AD5 rows already present; it maps to row 1's criterion rather than inventing a second one. AD1 gets no row — "a shell script, not model-emitted JSON" is satisfied by this epic existing at all, and a row asserting it would assert nothing. They are appended rather than inserted so rows 1–24 keep the numbers they were confirmed under.

**Rows 27 and 28 were added at Story 4's gate.** Running the roll-up against spec 44 showed that the matrices already carry `NFR1`–`NFR5` rows which no requirement record could ever match — untraced detection read the `## Functional Requirements` section only, so an untraced NFR was undetectable. That is a hole in the measurement spec 44 calls load-bearing, and it needed stating as a criterion rather than inheriting whatever the first parser happened to cover. Row 28 is story-originated: nothing in the spec says a Won't Have requirement must be excluded rather than reported untraced, but reporting one as a gap would report the spec working as intended as though it were a failure. Architecture decisions were deliberately left out — an AD is a choice the spec records, not a requirement to be delivered. Appended rather than inserted so rows 1–26 keep the numbers they were confirmed under.

**Rows 4 and 5** carry FR1's spec tag `[integration]` against story criteria tagged `[unit]`. Deliberate: the parsing half of FR1 is exercised in isolation at Story 2 and again through the assembled script at Story 3.

**Rows 13, 15, 16, 17 and 20–24** show `—` for Spec Test Approach. FR10 is a should-have, and NFR2, NFR3, AD3 and Test Infrastructure are not in spec 44's Acceptance Criteria Coverage table, which tags the must-haves plus NFR1 and the two Invariants. That is the spec being internally consistent rather than a coverage gap.
