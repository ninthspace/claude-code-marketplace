# A read that succeeds is not a read that is right

**Number**: 09  
**Source quick**: 11  
**Status**: complete — Synthesised from the five ungathered observations across quick records 09, 10 and 11 — the plan-narration fix and its aftermath, spanning 30 August to 2 September 2026. Four of the five are one failure mode across three categories: a check or a read that succeeds while pointed at the wrong thing. That pattern is visible only across the three records, which is the argument for one retro over the thread rather than one per record.
